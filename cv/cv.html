Profile

Storyboard artist who turns scripts into visual storytelling for film, television and advertising. I work mostly on children’s series and musicals, where clarity, humor and rhythm matter as much as the drawing, and I’m happiest when a board makes the whole team’s next step easier.

I started in 2D/3D generalist work on games, then moved into storyboards and animatics, from rough thumbnails to a final timed animatic. I keep software and processes efficient under tight deadlines and budgets, and I enjoy working closely with directors to shape narrative and visual language. Outside client work I make my own animatic series.
